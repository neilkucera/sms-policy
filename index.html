Second Brain SMS reminders

Second Brain is a self-hosted personal assistant application operated by Neil Kucera. It sends automated SMS reminders about upcoming deadlines (tax payments, insurance renewals, filings) and automated replies to questions the subscriber texts it.

To subscribe, text START to (858) 251-8938.

By texting START you agree to receive automated personal assistant reminder messages from Second Brain at the number you text from. Approximately 1 message per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

You will receive this confirmation: "Second Brain: You are now subscribed to personal assistant reminders from Neil Kucera. Approx 1 msg/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help."

This service has a single subscriber, its operator. Mobile numbers are never shared with third parties.
