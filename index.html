Fido Ops SMS reminders

Fido Ops is an internal operations notification application operated by Trash Caddies LLC (dba Fido), a Texas company owned by Neil Kucera. It sends automated SMS reminders about upcoming deadlines (tax payments, insurance renewals, filings) and automated replies to questions the subscriber texts it.

To subscribe, text START to (858) 251-8938.

By texting START you agree to receive automated operations reminder messages from Fido Ops at the number you text from. Approximately 1 message per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

You will receive this confirmation: "Fido Ops: You are now subscribed to operations reminders. Approx 1 msg/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help."

Recipients are limited to the company owner and authorized staff who opt in. Mobile numbers are never shared with third parties.
