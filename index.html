Neil Kucera SMS reminders

This is a personal assistant application operated by Neil Kucera, a sole proprietor in San Diego, California. It sends automated SMS reminders about upcoming deadlines (tax payments, insurance renewals, filings) and automated replies to questions the subscriber texts it. The only subscriber is the operator.

Reply STOP to cancel at any time, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

You will receive this confirmation: "Neil Kucera: You are now subscribed to personal assistant reminders. Approx 1 msg/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help."

Recipients are limited to the operator, who opts in by texting START. Mobile numbers are never shared with third parties.
