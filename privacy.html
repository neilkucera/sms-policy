SMS Privacy Policy

This SMS service ("Neil Kucera SMS Reminders") is operated by Neil Kucera, a sole proprietor in San Diego, California. The only recipient is the operator, who opts in by texting START.

Non-sharing. Mobile phone numbers and any information obtained through this service are not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party or affiliate for marketing or any other purpose. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.

Message frequency. Approximately one message per day, plus replies to questions the operator sends.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.

Effective September 2, 2026. See also the Terms and Conditions.
